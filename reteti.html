Reteti Elephant Sanctuary

A Safe Haven For Orphaned Elephants

(10/10 Rating)

EXPERIENCE CONSERVATION IN THE HEART OF NORTHERN KENYA

Reteti Elephant Sanctuary is a unique community-owned wildlife sanctuary located in the remote Namunyak Conservancy in northern Kenya. Dedicated to rescuing and rehabilitating orphaned and abandoned elephant calves, Reteti offers visitors an authentic and inspiring conservation experience deeply connected to local Samburu culture and community-led wildlife protection.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS: THE RETETI EXPERIENCE

Unlike traditional wildlife facilities, Reteti Elephant Sanctuary is the first indigenous community-owned elephant sanctuary in Africa, combining elephant rescue, conservation, and sustainable community involvement.

  • Elephant Rescue & Rehabilitation: The sanctuary cares for orphaned and injured baby elephants rescued from across northern Kenya. Visitors can witness the calves being bottle-fed and cared for by dedicated keepers as they recover and prepare for eventual release back into the wild.
  • Community-Led Conservation: Reteti stands as a powerful example of how local communities can lead wildlife conservation efforts. Managed and operated by the Samburu community, the sanctuary promotes coexistence between people and wildlife while creating employment and conservation awareness.
  • Close-Up Wildlife Encounters: Guests enjoy intimate viewing experiences with the elephants during feeding sessions and daily activities, providing exceptional photography and educational opportunities.
