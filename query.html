Popular Questions

Do I need travel insurance?

Travel insurance is strongly recommended for every trip. It should cover medical emergencies, cancellations, delays, and any adventure activities you take part in.

Do I need a visa for my trip?

Visa requirements vary by nationality and destination. Some countries offer eVisas, others have visa-on-arrival options. Check the latest rules before you travel.

When is the best time to travel?

The best season depends on your destination and desired experience. Dry season is usually best for safaris; low season can offer fewer crowds and better value.

Is it safe to travel?

Travel is generally safe when you book through trusted operators and follow local advice. Keep your belongings secure and stay aware of destination-specific conditions.

What payment methods are accepted?

Major cards are accepted in many places, but remote areas often prefer cash. Carry some USD, EUR, or local currency for tips and smaller purchases.

What’s included in my tour package?

Packages usually include accommodation, guided activities, park fees, and ground transport. Flights, extra meals, and optional excursions are normally excluded unless noted.

What happens if I need to cancel my trip?

Cancellation policies vary by tour. Refunds are often partial close to departure, so travel insurance is recommended to protect against unexpected changes.

What should I pack for my trip?

Packing depends on the trip type. For safaris, choose neutral clothing and sun protection. For trekking, bring warm layers and sturdy footwear.

What are the tipping guidelines?

Tipping is customary but optional. Typical amounts are $10-20 per person per day for guides, $5-10 per day for lodge staff, and $5-15 per porter.

Can I customize my trip?

Yes. Many tours can be tailored to your preferences, including luxury upgrades, private guides, extended stays, and extra activities.

Help Topics

By Destination

Which destinations are best for wildlife viewing?

East Africa’s top wildlife destinations include the Maasai Mara, Serengeti, Ngorongoro Crater, and Amboseli. Each offers unique viewing opportunities depending on the season.

When is the best time to visit each destination?

Dry season generally offers the best game viewing. For example, July–October is excellent for the Maasai Mara migration, while June–September is best for northern Tanzania.

Before You Travel

What documents do I need before departure?

Most visitors need a passport valid for at least six months, a return ticket, and the required visa. Some destinations also require proof of vaccinations.

How should I prepare my packing list?

Pack layers, neutral clothing, sun protection, and comfortable footwear. Don’t forget adapters, medications, and any specialized gear for adventure activities.

During Your Trip

What should I expect on a typical safari day?

Safari days usually start early with a morning game drive, followed by a break at lunch, and an afternoon or evening activity. Lodges provide meals and rest between drives.

How do I stay safe while on safari?

Follow your guide’s instructions, keep a safe distance from wildlife, and stay inside the vehicle unless told otherwise. Always respect local regulations and park rules.

After Your Trip

Can I request photos or trip notes after I return?

Yes, many operators provide trip summaries, photo packages, and travel notes after your safari. Contact us if you want a post-trip album or itinerary recap.

How do I share feedback or leave a review?

Feedback is welcome through our email or review platforms. We appreciate comments on guides, accommodations, and overall experience.

Health & Safety

What health precautions should I take?

Check vaccination requirements for your destination and bring any regular medications. Mosquito protection and hydration are key during safari travel.

Is it safe to travel during the rainy season?

Rainy season can still offer great travel experiences, with fewer crowds and lush scenery. However, some roads may be more challenging and certain areas may be less accessible.

Logistics & Travel Docs

What visa options are available?

Visas depend on your nationality and destination. Many countries offer eVisas or visa-on-arrival, but it’s best to confirm requirements before you travel.

How can I manage flight and transfer schedules?

We’ll coordinate your flights and ground transfers to match your itinerary. Arrivals and departures are planned to minimize travel stress and maximize your time on safari.

Adventure Specific

Can I add trekking or special activities to my itinerary?

Yes, many safaris can include mountain trekking, guided walks, and cultural experiences. We can tailor your trip based on activity level and interest.

What should I know about adventure-grade accommodation?

Adventure lodges often offer more rustic comfort, close connection to nature, and intimate settings. Expect quality service with an emphasis on authenticity.

Sustainability & Ethics

How do you support local communities?

We work with local guides, lodges, and community programs to ensure travel benefits the people and wildlife in the destination.

What practices are used to reduce environmental impact?

We promote responsible waste management, conservation fees, and lodges that use solar power, water-saving measures, and eco-friendly practices.
