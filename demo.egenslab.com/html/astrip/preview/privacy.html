Privacy Policy

YOUR PRIVACY MATTERS TO US

At Rasset Adventures, we are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information with transparency, integrity, and care.

By accessing our website or using our services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

To provide exceptional travel experiences and personalized services, we may collect certain personal information, including:

Personal Identification Information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Postal or billing address
  • Passport details (where required for bookings)
  • Emergency contact information

Booking and Payment Information

  • Reservation details
  • Travel preferences
  • Payment and transaction information
  • Special requests relating to your travel arrangements

Website and Communication Information

  • Information submitted through contact forms
  • Newsletter subscriptions
  • Customer feedback and reviews
  • Website usage data collected through cookies and analytics tools

How We Collect Your Information

We collect information when you:

  • Submit an inquiry through our website
  • Request a quotation
  • Book a safari, tour, or travel service
  • Subscribe to our newsletter
  • Contact us via email, telephone, social media, or messaging platforms
  • Participate in surveys, promotions, or marketing campaigns
  • Interact with our website

How We Use Your Information

Deliver Travel Services

  • Process and manage bookings
  • Confirm reservations and travel arrangements
  • Coordinate accommodation, transportation, and activities
  • Provide customer support before, during, and after your trip

Improve Our Services

We use information to enhance website functionality, analyze preferences, and improve the quality of our tours and services.

Communication and Marketing

With your consent, we may send travel inspiration, special offers, newsletters, and company news. You may unsubscribe at any time.

Sharing Your Information

Rasset Adventures respects your privacy and will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information. However, we may share relevant information with trusted travel partners such as:

  • Hotels and lodges
  • Airlines and transport providers
  • National parks and conservation authorities
  • Activity operators and guides
  • Payment processing providers

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical, organizational, and administrative measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means, no online transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

International Data Transfers

Certain travel arrangements may require us to share information with service providers outside your country of residence. We take reasonable steps to ensure protection and compliance with applicable data protection laws.

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill travel and contractual obligations, comply with legal requirements, resolve disputes, and maintain business records. When information is no longer required, it is securely deleted or anonymized.

Your Rights

Depending on applicable data protection laws, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal information, withdraw consent, and request a copy of the data we hold about you.

Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve functionality, enhance user experience, and analyze website performance. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

Rasset Adventures reserves the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and continued use of our website constitutes acceptance of those changes.
