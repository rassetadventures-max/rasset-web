Accommodation Options

Experience comfortable, authentic lodges like Springlands Hotel and Serengeti Safari Lodge. These locations offer swimming pools, beautiful dining areas with local and continental dishes, and completely en-suite rooms with modern amenities.

For an upgraded experience, we offer stays at premium luxury camps like Ole Serai Luxury Camp or Gran Melia Hotel. These feature five-star service, infinity pools, spa treatments, and the absolute best vantage points over the savanna.

Travel Plan (4 Days, 3 Nights Options)

01

OPTION 1: DIANI BEACH RETREAT

Day 1: Arrival in Paradise

Upon landing at Diani Airport (Ukunda), you will be met by our driver and whisked away to your beachfront resort. After check-in, the rest of the day is yours to kick off your shoes and feel the soft, white sand between your toes.

  • Meal Plan: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
  • Accommodation: Diani Sea Lodge, Diani Sea Resort, or similar

Day 2: Sun, Sand, and Sea

Wake up to the sound of the waves. Today is entirely yours to define. Whether you want to lounge by the pool with a cocktail, book a glass-bottom boat tour to the sandbar, or try your hand at kite surfing, the day is an open canvas.

  • Meal Plan: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
  • Accommodation: Diani Sea Lodge, Diani Sea Resort, or similar

Day 3: Deep Relaxation

Spend another day soaking in the magnificent views of the Indian Ocean. Diani's lush coastal forest often brings visits from local Colobus monkeys, providing a unique backdrop to your tropical retreat. Take a sunset stroll along the shore to cap off your final night.

  • Meal Plan: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
  • Accommodation: Diani Sea Lodge, Diani Sea Resort, or similar

Day 4: Farewell to the Coast

After a leisurely breakfast and perhaps a final morning swim, your driver will pick you up at your preferred time for your transfer back to Diani Airport for your departure flight.

  • Meal Plan: Breakfast, Lunch(Optional)
  • Accommodation: NONE
02

OPTION 2: ZANZIBAR ESCAPE

Day 1: Arrival in the Spice Island

Welcome to Zanzibar! Upon arrival at Zanzibar International Airport, you will be greeted by your driver and transferred to the northern tip of the island. Check into your hotel in the Kendwa area, famous for its non-tidal beachesmeaning you can swim all day long.

  • Meal Plan: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
  • Accommodation: Hotel Riu Palace Zanzibar, Kendwa Rocks Beach Resort, or similar

Day 2: Tropical Bliss at Kendwa

Spend the day exactly how you choose. Lounge on the powdery white sands, take a dip in the crystal-clear Indian Ocean, or explore the coral reefs. Kendwa is renowned for its vibrant atmosphere and breathtaking sunsetsthe perfect backdrop for a relaxed evening.

  • Meal Plan: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
  • Accommodation: Hotel Riu Palace Zanzibar, Kendwa Rocks Beach Resort, or similar

Day 3: The Magic of Stone Town

After a final seaside breakfast, we transfer you to the historic heart of the island: Stone Town. Spend the afternoon getting lost in its labyrinthine narrow streets, admiring the famous carved Zanzibar doors, and soaking in the scent of cloves and cinnamon from the local markets.

  • Meal Plan: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
  • Accommodation: Tembo House Hotel, Maru Maru Hotel, or similar

Day 4: Farewell Zanzibar

Enjoy a final morning taking in the views of the harbor or doing some last-minute shopping for local textiles and spices. Your driver will meet you at your hotel at your preferred time for your transfer back to Zanzibar Airport for your journey home.

  • Meal Plan: Breakfast, Lunch(Optional)
  • Accommodation: NONE

Location Map

Diani Beach is located on Kenya's southeastern Indian Ocean coast, approximately 30 km south of Mombasa city, easily reached via Ukunda airstrip or road from Mombasa. Zanzibar is an autonomous archipelago off Tanzania's coast, with its main island Unguja (commonly called Zanzibar) located roughly 75 km north of Dar es Salaam. The two beach destinations are approximately 350 km apart across open water, connected by direct flights from Mombasa or Nairobi to Zanzibar's Abeid Amani Karume International Airport, with flight times of about 1â€“1.5 hours. This proximity makes combining Diani's powder-white sands with Zanzibar's spice-scented Stone Town and turquoise waters a seamless "dual-beach" East African itinerary.

The Indian Ocean is the third-largest of the world's five oceanic divisions. It extends from the eastern coast of Africa to the western coast of Australia, and from the southern tip of India to the Southern Ocean. The Indian Ocean is the warmest ocean in the world, with an average surface temperature of 28.4 degrees Celsius. It is also the saltiest ocean, with an average salinity of 35 parts per thousand.
