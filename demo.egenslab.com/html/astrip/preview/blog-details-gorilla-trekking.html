Gorilla trekking in Uganda: what to expect on a primate safari

  • May 10, 2026
  • Rasset Adventures

Gorilla trekking in Uganda is a once-in-a-lifetime experience, offering close encounters with mountain gorillas in Bwindi Impenetrable Forest or Mgahinga Gorilla National Park. Treks are guided, permit-based, and limited to small groups.

What to bring

  • Sturdy hiking boots, waterproof jacket, and long-sleeve layers
  • Anti-malarial protection and insect repellent
  • Camera with good stabilisation for low-light forest conditions

Rasset Adventures can arrange your permit, local guide, and comfortable lodging to make the trek safe and memorable.
