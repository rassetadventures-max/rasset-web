Why the Maasai Mara is Kenya’s iconic wildlife destination

  • June 1, 2026
  • Rasset Adventures

The Maasai Mara is the heart of Kenya’s safari country, famed for dramatic predator sightings, wide-open savannahs and world-class wildlife viewing. The annual Great Migration brings millions of wildebeest and zebra through the reserve.

Best times to visit

  • July to October for migration and excellent game viewing
  • June and November for quieter game drives and green scenery
  • Early morning drives offer the best chance to see lions, cheetah and elephant
