About Us

Where Luxury Meets Adventure

Discover the beauty of East Africa with Rasset Adventures where unforgettable wildlife encounters, breathtaking landscapes, rich cultures, and luxury experiences come together. We create tailor-made safaris and adventures designed to inspire, excite, and leave lasting memories across Kenya, Tanzania, Zanzibar, Uganda, and beyond.

RASSET ADVENTURES was founded five years ago by Steve.A , a Kenyan-born travel enthusiast with a deep-rooted passion for exploration, nature, and African landscapes. Growing up surrounded by Kenya's rich natural beauty, Steve developed an early curiosity for travel through small hiking experiences and outdoor adventures, which gradually grew into a lifelong calling. His passion for discovery led him to pursue formal studies in Wildlife and Tourism Management, where he gained a strong academic foundation in conservation, safari operations, and sustainable tourism practices. While studying, he actively continued exploring East Africa, visiting numerous national parks, cultural sites, and remote destinations across the region.

Driven by a desire to truly understand Africa's diverse landscapes, Steve challenged himself by conquering some of the continent's most iconic peaks, including Mount Kilimanjaro in Tanzania, Ol Doinyo Lengai in Tanzania, and Mount Kenya in Kenya. These experiences not only strengthened his resilience and leadership but also deepened his appreciation for Africa's natural heritage. Inspired by these journeys, he envisioned a company that would go beyond ordinary tours one that would connect travelers to authentic African experiences while promoting conservation, culture, and community engagement. This vision led to the birth of Rasset Adventures.

Our Story

From a passion for wildlife to curated safari journeys

Rasset Adventures began with a love for East Africa's wild places and a commitment to responsible travel. Founded by STEVE 5 years ago, the company started small, guided by local expertise, and grew into a trusted name for travelers seeking authentic safaris, cultural immersion, and personalized service.

Over the years, Rasset Adventures has grown into a trusted travel brand known for personalized service, unforgettable safaris, and immersive cultural experiences. Every itinerary we create is carefully designed to celebrate the beauty of the land, respect local communities, and protect the incredible wildlife that makes East Africa truly extraordinary.

Today, we design every itinerary to honor the land, the people, and the incredible wildlife that make East Africa and the region so special. Our story is rooted in meaningful experiences and a promise to help you explore with confidence and care.
